HOLLY BOT

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

Holly Bot is an AI assistant app where you create bots, chat with them and let them work for you, using AI providers you choose with your own API keys. What you keep in Holly Bot is stored in your own account, which only you can open. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your account and what it holds

Holly Bot requires an account. Everything the app keeps for you is stored in it: your bots and their settings, your chats and messages, your bots' memories, files you or your bots save or attach, routines, tasks between bots, activity logs, usage counts, your profile (the name and details you choose to share with your bots) and your settings, including the API keys you add for AI providers and other services.

That data is held for us by Convex, the hosting provider that runs our backend, in the United States. It is kept so that you can sign in on any device and find the same bots and chats. Each account can read and change only its own data; we enforce that on our server, not only in the app.

We use your data only to run the app for you. We do not read it, analyse it, sell it, or share it with anyone, and we do not use it to train AI models.

The app on your device also keeps your sign-in session and, until they reach our server, any changes made while you were offline. Signing out sends those changes and removes them from the device.

Signing in

You sign in with Apple or with Google. We store the email address they give us, the name they share and, with Google, your profile picture address, along with an identifier for your Apple or Google account, so we can recognise you the next time you sign in and show who is signed in.

Sign in with Apple shares your name only the first time you use it. If you choose Apple's Hide My Email, we receive a relay address instead of your real one, and that is the address we store. Apple and Google handle sign-in under their own privacy terms.

AI providers

When a bot works, the app on your device sends what the bot needs directly to the AI provider you chose for it, using your own API key: the conversation, the bot's instructions and relevant memories, your profile, and any files or images involved. These requests do not pass through our server, and we do not see them.

Each provider handles what it receives under its own terms and privacy policy, which you accept when you create a key with them. The providers Holly Bot works with include DeepSeek (the default for new bots, whose services are operated from the People's Republic of China), Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Google Gemini, Mistral, Groq and OpenRouter, as well as a local model on your own computer or any other service you set up. Please read the policy of each provider you use.

Web search, reading pages and other tools

When a bot searches the web, the search is sent to the service you set up for it (Tavily, Exa or Jina) with your key. When a bot reads a web page, the page's address is sent to Jina's reader service to fetch it, or the page is fetched directly. The first time a bot runs Python code, the Python runtime is downloaded from the jsDelivr CDN; the code itself runs on your device. Icons shown beside the sources a bot cites are loaded from Google's website-icon service, which receives those websites' addresses.

Plugins (MCP servers) and other services you connect receive what a bot sends them while using them.

Holly Computer

Holly Computer is an optional companion you can run on your own PC or Mac so your bots live there. When you use it, your bots, chats, memories, files and keys are stored on that computer, not in your Holly Bot account, and the bots act on it with the permissions you give them. Your phone reaches it through a Cloudflare tunnel or your Wi-Fi network, protected by a pairing token that stays on your devices.

Voice

Dictation uses your device's or browser's speech recognition, which may send your voice to Apple or Google to turn it into text. Read-aloud uses the voices built into your device. Holly Bot does not record or store audio.

Notifications

Notifications, if you turn them on, are shown by the app on your device. We do not run a push notification service.

What we never do

We do not run analytics, telemetry or advertising. We do not sell, rent or share personal information, we load no third-party trackers, and we do not build a profile of you. The app itself is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, processes the network information needed to deliver it.

Deleting your data

To remove your account and everything in it, open Settings and choose Delete Account. It erases your bots, chats, memories, files, routines, settings and API keys, and your sign-in details, straight away. It cannot be undone.

To erase what's in your account but keep the account, open Settings, then Data & Backup, then Erase all data. Signing out deletes nothing; your account is there when you sign in again.

Bots kept on a Holly Computer live on that computer; delete them there. You can also ask us to delete your account by email.

Children

Holly Bot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app.
